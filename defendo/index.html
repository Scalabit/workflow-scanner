Features

Everything you need to stay secure

A complete security platform designed for the realities of SME IT — powerful enough for your security team, simple enough for everyone else.

Real-Time Device Monitoring

Continuous visibility across every endpoint — laptops, servers, mobiles, and IoT devices — with live health and connectivity status.

Centralized Log Management

All device events, system logs, and network activity flow into one searchable, tamper-evident repository — with 12-month retention.

AI-Powered Threat Detection

Machine learning models trained on millions of threat patterns identify anomalies and zero-day behaviours in real time, without signature files.

Automated Policy Enforcement

Define security rules once; let AI agents enforce them automatically — quarantining devices, blocking connections, or alerting your team instantly.

Instant Alerts & Reports

Push notifications, email digests, and executive-ready PDF reports delivered on your schedule — so you're always informed, never overwhelmed.

Compliance Ready

Built-in frameworks for GDPR, ISO 27001, and NIS2. Generate audit trails, evidence packs, and compliance dashboards with a single click.

How It Works

Up and running in under 30 minutes

No complex infrastructure required. Defendo deploys via a lightweight agent and starts protecting your business immediately.

1

Connect Your Devices

Install the Defendo agent on Windows, macOS, or Linux in minutes. Group devices by team, location, or risk level from the dashboard.

2

Monitor & Collect Logs

Every event is captured, normalised, and streamed to your centralised log store — encrypted in transit and at rest. Full audit trail from day one.

3

AI Detects & Responds

Our AI agents analyse behaviour 24/7. When a threat is detected, automated playbooks respond immediately — isolate, alert, remediate.
